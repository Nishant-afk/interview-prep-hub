Each card shows a question. Recall the answer, flip the card, then grade yourself honestly:

- **Again** puts the card back in box 1. You will see it again today.
- **Good** moves it up one box. Boxes come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
- **Easy** moves it up two boxes.
