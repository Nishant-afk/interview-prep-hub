## How this is asked

Interviewers check whether you have moved with the framework: signals vs RxJS, OnPush and zoneless change detection, `@defer`, functional guards and interceptors. RxJS flattening operators (switchMap vs mergeMap vs concatMap vs exhaustMap) are asked almost every time.
