## How to use this site

1. Read a topic and the **Interview Q&A** at the bottom. Try to answer each question out loud before expanding it.
2. Take the **quick quiz** at the end of the page, then click **Mark as studied**.
3. Once a section is done, take its **section test** in the Test Centre. Aim for 80% or more.
4. Weekly, take a **mock test** and drill **weak areas** until that list is empty.

Progress and test history are stored in your browser. Export them from the Test Centre history tab to move between devices.
